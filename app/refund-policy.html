Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

Return window

Eligible items can be requested for return within 3 days from the delivery date. Products should be unused, in their original condition, and returned with original packaging where applicable.

How to request a return

Email MARKET HUB support with your order details, the reason for return and clear photos if the item is damaged, defective or incorrect. We will review the request and guide you on the next step.

Refunds

After an approved return is received and checked, eligible refunds are credited to the MARKET HUB Wallet. Wallet refunds are valid for 12 months from the date of credit.

Non-returnable items

Some products may not be returnable for hygiene, safety or clearance reasons. Any such restriction is shown on the product page or at checkout.

Contact

For return or refund help, contact MARKET HUB support.
