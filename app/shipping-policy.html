Shipping Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

Delivery time

Most orders are expected to be delivered within 7–8 days after order confirmation. Delivery time can vary by PIN code, product availability, weather, public holidays and logistics conditions.

Shipping partner and tracking

MARKET HUB uses delivery partners, including Delhivery, to ship eligible orders. Tracking details are shared when the shipment is dispatched.

Shipping charges

Any shipping charge, Cash on Delivery charge or offer is shown before payment at checkout. Charges can depend on the delivery location and order value.

Delivery address

Please provide a complete, accurate address and working mobile number. Delivery may be delayed or cancelled if the address, phone number or PIN code is incorrect.
